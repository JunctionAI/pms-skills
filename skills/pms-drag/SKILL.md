# Name obstructing forces

/pms-drag — Name what’s getting in its way.

Phase: **02 Understand**. See what drives it.

Also invoked as `/drag`.

## When
The story blames mood, luck, or character instead of structure.

## Reads
Observations tagged to the current goal or structure.

## Writes
Blocking forces. Empty is allowed: no specific barrier established.

## Steps
1. Name structural blockers, not character flaws.
2. If no specific barrier is established, say so.

## Will not do
- Will not diagnose personality, luck, or moral failure.

## Output
## Drag
- blocking: []
- relative_to:
- none_established: true|false

## Example
`/pms-drag`

## Doctrine
Observe. Understand. Navigate. Align.

- Provenance: reported | observed | inferred | not established.
- Do not invent progress, probability, or completion percentages.
- Empty is allowed. Gaps stay gaps.
- Forward/drag are relative to a named criterion or destination.
- If live context is not actually available, mark it **missing**.
