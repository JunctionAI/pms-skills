# Map a structure

/pms-map — Map a structure: what it is, what drives it, what helps, what gets in its way.

Phase: **01 Observe**. Look at what is there.

Also invoked as `/map`.

## When
A situation exists and you have not yet named the structure underneath it.

## Reads
Destination (optional), present-tense reports, any attached evidence.

## Writes
A first structural frame: core, forces, unknowns. Labeled provisional.

## Steps
1. Name the structure in one sentence (the core, not the costume).
2. List helping forces (forward) and obstructing forces (drag). Empty is allowed.
3. Name unknowns. Do not fill them.
4. Mark the whole reading **provisional**.

## Will not do
- Will not treat a vibe, brand, or event as the structure.
- Will not invent forces that were not in the input or evidence.

## Output
## Structure
- core:
- forward: []
- drag: []
- unknowns: []
- provenance: provisional

## Example
`/pms-map my relationship with work after the promotion`

## Doctrine
Observe. Understand. Navigate. Align.

- Provenance: reported | observed | inferred | not established.
- Do not invent progress, probability, or completion percentages.
- Empty is allowed. Gaps stay gaps.
- Forward/drag are relative to a named criterion or destination.
- If live context is not actually available, mark it **missing**.
