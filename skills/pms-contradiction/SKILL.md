# Core contradiction

/pms-contradiction — Surface the core contradiction, if the evidence supports one.

Phase: **02 Understand**. See what drives it.

Also invoked as `/contradiction`.

## When
Two operating rules cannot both be true and the structure is stalling.

## Reads
Forward vs drag, cost vs benefit, stated goal vs protected bounds.

## Writes
One contradiction, or none established.

## Steps
1. Find two rules that cannot both hold.
2. If you cannot point at both in the frame, none established.

## Will not do
- Will not manufacture drama or a 'shadow' narrative.

## Output
## Contradiction
- a:
- b:
- established: true|false

## Example
`/pms-contradiction`

## Doctrine
Observe. Understand. Navigate. Align.

- Provenance: reported | observed | inferred | not established.
- Do not invent progress, probability, or completion percentages.
- Empty is allowed. Gaps stay gaps.
- Forward/drag are relative to a named criterion or destination.
- If live context is not actually available, mark it **missing**.
